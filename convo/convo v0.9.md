Jimmy's fifth round of feedback, and what changed.

Then one step where the row gets bigger with an = side, showing the unsorted answer. After that, the steps repair and regroup the answer to follow the rules.

4. **Rules.** On the left, the full rule description, in Jimmy's own words from convo v0.1. On the right, the examples.

6. **Looks.** Notebook is out. Candy needs the Roman numerals in a serif font. Three more looks, with more change to the layout: tabs and panels placed differently, and the scale of the screen changed.
7. **Quiz.** IL, IC and VIX are false, not ambiguous. They are not well written.
8. **Settings.** Add more settings that might help. Jimmy will review which to keep.

## What changed

- **Add & Subtract.** The whole sum stays on one line. Parts are expanded in place, with an arrow. Matching parts are crossed out on both sides. Any pairs left are broken. An = sign then appears, with the unsorted answer to its right. Then the answer is grouped one step at a time and written in simplest form. The board is gone.
- **Rules.** Five rules on the left, with Jimmy's full wording. Rule 4 says "Coming later" and has no examples.
- **Letters.** Pairs that exist, and pairs that never exist. The past-1000 box shows only when the setting is on.
- **Quiz.** IL, IC and VIX are False. "Ambiguous" is removed.
- **Looks.** Sunny, Chalk, Candy (serif numerals), Poster (black tab bar stuck to the top), Folder (tabs sit on the panels), Garden (narrow centred column, round tiles). Notebook is removed.
- **Settings.** Text size, step speed (slow, normal, quick), calm motion (no animation), letters past 1000, plus the earlier options.
- **Long Ago.** Pebbles slide onto the board when a panel opens. The timeline shows each date as a plain age ("About 1,400 years ago") with a short sentence.
