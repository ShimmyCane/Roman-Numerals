## What changed

- **Regrouping happens on one equation line, with no before → after boxes.** Every row is just the equation: `LXXXV − LXVI = ?`.
  - **Take away in one move:** every letter (or short form) that both numbers have is crossed out on both sides at once. "Both numbers have L, X and V. Take them away: cross them out on both sides."
  - **Trades stay, one per row, in the equation itself.** The traded letter fades and its pieces take its place, over an underline. The last X goes first. "Not enough I's. Trade one X for V + V." Then "Trade one V for I + I + I + I + I."
  - **Short forms open only when needed**, also in the equation (IX fades, V I I I I appears underlined).
  - **The =:** the left side is in focus until everything in the second number is crossed out. Then what is left is written after the = sign, the right side comes into focus, and regrouping happens there ("VIIII is the long way. The short way is IX."). The last row shows `LXXXV − LXVI = XIX` with a check mark.
- **Adding works the same way:** open short forms only when needed, put all the letters together after the = sign, biggest first, then regroup there. X + LXXV slots straight in as LXXXV in one move.
- **Longer sums** (XL + XX − IX) start with the whole sum and "Work two numbers at a time", then do each pair on its own line. The last row shows the whole sum with its answer.
- **Narrow screens:** each line shrinks to fit. A numeral as written never breaks. Only a working pile of letters (after = before regrouping, or a number already being worked on) may break, and only where the letter changes, as a last resort on small phones.
- The counting board (in settings) and the Regroup tab are unchanged.
- **Checked:** every difference up to M (a sample past CCC) and every sum up to DC + DC (a sample past CC) ends at the simplest form, with all of the second number crossed out. 37 logic tests pass.
